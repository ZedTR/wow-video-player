Privacy Policy for Waiting On My Wife

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Waiting On My Wife ("the app") is developed by an independent developer ("we," "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary: The app has no backend server, no user accounts, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. Everything the app stores is kept on your device. The only outside services it talks to are YouTube's, for playing and refreshing videos.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive, or have access to any information from your use of the app. Specifically:

  • We do not operate a server. There is nothing for your data to be sent to.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services.
  • We do not require or support account creation, sign-in, or any personal identifiers.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally, on your device only:

Data Purpose Where it's stored
Wait-time tallies (per category and total) Show your running totals on the home screen Local device storage (UserDefaults on iOS / SharedPreferences on Android), via the app's on-device settings storage
Your response to the video-content disclaimer (accept/decline) and its timestamp Remember whether you've agreed to watch YouTube content, and maintain a local log of that choice A local file in the app's own document storage on your device

None of this data is transmitted anywhere. It is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app, and the in-app reset controls let you clear the wait-time tallies at any time.

Local notifications

The app schedules reminder notifications (e.g., at 30/60/90/120 minutes into a wait) using your device's own notification system. These are generated and scheduled entirely on-device; no notification content or scheduling data is sent to us or to any third party.

Third-party service: YouTube

To provide the "watch a video while you wait" feature, the app embeds YouTube's official IFrame Player and pulls public video listings from YouTube channel RSS feeds. When a video loads:

  • The video is played through YouTube's own player (owned and operated by Google LLC), not by us.
  • YouTube/Google may collect information about that playback — such as device information, IP address, cookies, and viewing activity — under their own privacy policy, not ours. We do not have access to this data.
  • We do not use the paid/authenticated YouTube Data API and do not attach any personal identifiers to these requests; the RSS feeds and video player are fetched anonymously, the same as visiting a public YouTube page in a browser.

You can review how Google/YouTube handles this data here:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

Before any video plays, the app shows a disclaimer explaining that video content is pulled from YouTube, is not reviewed or controlled by us, and requires your agreement to continue. You can decline, and you can revisit that choice at any time from within the app.

Sharing

If you use the app's share feature, it hands off to your device's native share sheet (e.g., Messages, Mail, or other apps you choose). What happens to the shared content after that is governed by the destination app you pick — it does not pass through us.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because the app embeds third-party YouTube content that we do not control or curate beyond selecting source channels, it is not intended for use by young children without parental supervision.

Data retention and deletion

Because nothing is collected or stored by us, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. All app data lives on your device and is removed when you clear it in-app or uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time (for example, if the app's functionality changes). Changes will be posted at this same location with an updated effective date.
